## What it is

A **constraint** is a hard limit the solution must respect (budget, platform, regulation, timeline).

## How it fits

Constraints sit under **Guardrails**. Unlike assumptions, they are not optional bets — features and architecture should honour them.

## Guidance

- Write constraints as measurable or observable limits.
- Separate business policy (rules) from technical/hard limits (constraints).
- Surface conflicting constraints early with stakeholders.
