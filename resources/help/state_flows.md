## What it is

A **state flow** diagram shows lifecycle or status transitions for an entity or process.

## How it fits

State flows sit under **Diagrams**. Use them when the question is “what states exist and how do we move between them?”

## Guidance

- Name states in business language, not only UI labels.
- Call out invalid or forbidden transitions explicitly when useful.
- Keep one primary subject per diagram.
