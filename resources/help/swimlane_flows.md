## What it is

A **swimlane flow** shows a process across actors or organisational lanes — who does what, in what order.

## How it fits

Swimlane flows sit under **Diagrams**. Pair them with state flows (lifecycle) and C4 (structure) for a fuller picture.

## Guidance

- One lane per actor or responsible party.
- Focus on the happy path first, then exceptions.
- Use workshops to validate handoffs between lanes.
- Each process and decision step gets a stable **Identifier** (`PS-n`) on save.
- Use **Satisfy** to link a process/decision step to the Functional Requirement or BDD Feature it implements. Unlinked steps appear as gaps in the Traceability Matrix.
