## What it is

A **swimlane flow** shows a process across actors or organisational lanes — who does what, in what order. In BAssist it is the Business Process Diagram (BPD) surface.

## How it fits

Swimlane flows sit under **Diagrams**. Pair them with state flows (lifecycle) and C4 (structure) for a fuller picture. Process and decision steps are first-class requirements-adjacent rows (`PS-n`) that can link upstream to a Stakeholder Need; Functional Requirements and BDD Features optionally point back to a step for BPD coverage.

## Guidance

- One lane per actor or responsible party.
- Optionally assign a **Lane color** (pastel palette) and an **Element color** (richer mid-tone palette) so actors and steps are easier to distinguish in the diagram.
- Use **Color mode** to apply both, lanes alone, or elements alone in the rendered diagram (saved color choices are kept either way).
- Click **Print diagram** to open the current diagram preview in a new window (diagram only) and raise the browser Print dialog, where you can also save a PDF. Works on unsaved edits.
- Focus on the happy path first, then exceptions.
- Use workshops to validate handoffs between lanes.
- Each process and decision step gets a stable project-scoped **Identifier** (`PS-n`) on save.
- Link process/decision steps to the **Stakeholder Need** they support. On FR and Feature forms, optionally select the process step they elaborate.
- Steps without a Stakeholder Need, or without an elaborating FR/Feature, appear as gaps in the Traceability Matrix and readiness checklist.
