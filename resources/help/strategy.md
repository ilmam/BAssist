## What it is

**Strategy** covers the project’s current state, future state, change approach, and what is in or out of scope.

## How it fits

Strategy frames the need spine: it explains *why* the work exists and which boundaries guardrails and scope items enforce.

## Guidance

- Capture current vs future state before listing many scope items.
- Keep scope items explicit (in vs out) so debates stay concrete.
- Align readiness and traceability gaps with the strategic baseline.
