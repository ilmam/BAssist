## What it is

**Strategy** is the project’s Strategy Analysis package: the **Strategic Baseline** narrative (current state, future state, change strategy) plus **Scope Items** that mark what is in or out of solution scope.

## How it fits

Strategy frames the need spine. The baseline explains *why* the work exists and how change will happen; Scope Items and guardrails (assumptions, constraints, risks) keep boundaries concrete. Business Objectives and Business Needs carry measurable outcomes and problems—the baseline should stay consistent with them, not replace them.

## Guidance

- Capture **Current state** / **Future state** / **Change strategy** on the Strategic Baseline before listing many scope items or diving into RADD.
- Keep **Scope Items** explicit (In vs Out) so debates stay concrete.
- Move status to **Approved** only after stakeholder consensus; after that, use **Change Requests** and **Traceability** instead of silent edits.
- Use **Readiness & Gaps** (dashboard help) for enterprise readiness, gap analysis, and solution vs enterprise limitations under Task 6.4 / 8.3–8.4.
- Open the entity help on **Strategic Baseline** or **Scope** for field-level guidance.
