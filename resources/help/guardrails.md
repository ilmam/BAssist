## What it is

**Guardrails** are assumptions, constraints, and business rules that surround the need spine and limit or guide solution choices.

## How it fits

They protect delivery from hidden risk: open assumptions soften readiness; constraints and rules must be respected by features and architecture.

## Guidance

- Separate beliefs (assumptions) from hard limits (constraints) and policies (rules).
- Revisit open assumptions regularly with owners.
- Link rules to the stories or features they affect when known.
