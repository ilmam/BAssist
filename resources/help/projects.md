## What it is

A **project** is the delivery container for BA artifacts: needs, features, guardrails, strategy, diagrams, and exports.

## How it fits

Work is scoped to a project (often under a workspace). Dashboards and readiness summarise progress across the need spine.

## Guidance

- Confirm the active project before creating artifacts.
- Use readiness as guidance, not a hard gate.
- Keep codes and names stable so exports and links stay useful.
